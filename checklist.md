# THE LADDER - afternoon to 270

This is the primary objective. Every run leads with it.

## Why the afternoon

Mornings are at bar capacity. 8-10AM runs 25-31 orders per hour. There is no headroom there.
Afternoon 2-6PM does roughly **51 orders total across four hours** (5PM ~4.7, 6PM ~0.9), barely
more than the single 9AM hour. The whole gap to 270 lives in that window.

Saturdays already run 256-268. The gap is **weekday afternoons**, Tue-Fri.

## The rungs

| Rung | Afternoon 2-6PM tx/day | Implied daytime tx/day | Play |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (baseline) | 51 | ~218 | starting point |
| 1 | 70 | ~237 | afternoon product/social drops, 2-3PM Tue-Thu |
| 2 | 90 | ~257 | 3-6PM happy hour promo pricing |
| 3 | 103 | **270** | 4-6PM aperitivo bridge into Night at Boxx |

Sequenced over roughly 8 weeks from Jul 16, 2026. The original Jun 28 deadline was missed and is
retired. Do not reference it as live.

## How to report against it

Every run, report the **trailing 14-day 2-6PM average**, day-of-week matched against the prior
14 days, and place it on the rung ladder. State the current rung, the distance to the next rung in
transactions per day, and whether the trend over the last three runs is up, flat, or down.

Never report afternoon progress from a contaminated window without saying so.

## Margin guardrail

Play 2 is discount-based and can raise transaction count while destroying contribution. Alongside
every afternoon count, report **net sales per transaction** for the 2-6PM window and compare it to
the trailing four-week non-promo weekday figure. If it has fallen more than 8%, that is the lead
finding of the run, ahead of the transaction count.

Count without margin is not progress.

## The demand-creation caveat

The only thing that has demonstrably created new demand at Boxx is an organic product plus social
moment: the Competition Coffees launch (Jul 2) and the viral Instagram post (Jul 5) drove DTC from
$71/day to a $241/day peak, about 3.4x, roughly $1,200 incremental over 11 days, with the effect
landing on mix and basket more than on transaction count.

Ads capture existing demand. They have not created it here. When recommending, weight
product/social/daypart levers above ad-account levers unless the ad account has an actual defect.
